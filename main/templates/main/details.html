FAQ's

When should I RSVP by?

Please RSVP no later than 01/08/2026 using the QR code on your formal invitation or by clicking the RSVP tab in the menu above. If you do not RSVP in time, we will assume that you are unable to attend.

What do I do if I can't attend?

You will be missed! If you can not make it to the wedding, please let us know as soon as possible and RSVP "not attending" so we can plan accordingly. Thank you!

Can I take pictures during the ceremony?

NO. We are having an unplugged ceremony, meaning that once the ceremony begins, we ask that all phones be put away and silenced. We want everyone to be fully present with us and for our photographer to be able to capture the best moments, which we will absolutely share with you. Post ceremony, please feel free to take any pictures or videos.

Can I bring children?

We have chosen to make our wedding an adults only event. For this reason we are unable to accommodate children and kindly request they are not in attendance. We hope you understand and are still able to make it!

Can I bring a guest?

Due to limited space, we are only able to accommodate those guests formally invited. Your unique code provided on the invite will list those invited to the ceremony on the RSVP section of the website.

Wedding Gifts

Your presence on our special day truly is the greatest gift of all. However, if you would like to give a gift, we would be incredibly grateful for a contribution towards our honeymoon fund. You can find more details on our Wedding Registry page.

Parking

There is parking at the venue on a first come first served basis but it should be large enough to accommodate all guests.
